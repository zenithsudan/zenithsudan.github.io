ABOUT US

Why Zenith exists

Zenith Engineering is a Sudanese engineering and construction company, registered in Kassala in 2025. It was founded by engineers who have delivered projects from the inside. Quality and timing depend on the technical team on site, and clients lose time coordinating separate contractors for building, water, energy and design. We offer one company, one standard and a trusted local team.

Consistent quality

The same standard on every site, checked at each stage of the work.

Reliable commitment

Specialized teams

Dedicated crews for civil, water, solar and design work.

Modern practice

BIM, CAD, hydraulic modelling and GIS applied to reduce errors before work reaches site.

WATER SYSTEMS

From source to tap, built to last

Tank fabrication and installation

SOLAR AND CONSULTANCY

Reliable power, clear designs

Solar energy

We design, supply and install solar systems sized to the real load, so facilities keep running through outages and fuel shortages: homes and offices with battery storage, solar water pumping, and power for irrigation and farm facilities.

Engineering consultancy

We prepare the drawings, designs and cost estimates that tender, budget and build decisions depend on, and assess existing buildings before money is spent on repairs. Our tools: AutoCAD, Civil 3D, EPANET, GIS and BIM (Revit).

METHODOLOGY AND QUALITY

The same standard on every site

Quality at every stage

Materials checked on delivery, works inspected before they are covered, tests recorded before handover.

Health and safety

Hazards identified before work starts, with protective equipment, safe methods and incident reporting.

Integrity

Honest bidding, declared conflicts of interest, and no tolerance for bribery or improper payments.

Safeguarding

A code of conduct for all staff and subcontractors, a named focal point and clear steps for raising concerns.

Our water and construction work is planned in line with Sphere standards and the Core Humanitarian Standard, and our safeguarding approach follows IASC principles. We work with community participation, coordinate with partners and design for operation and maintenance after handover.
